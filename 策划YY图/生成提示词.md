## 风格说明

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: single 3D environment or prop concept frame for a Roblox underground shelter design sheet. Match the supplied BLD interior art direction observed in this chat: grounded stylized game render, wide architectural framing around 3:2, beveled low-poly modular geometry with realistic material response, warm amber strip lights set into worn warm-grey concrete and ivory painted steel, dark charcoal exposed infrastructure, dusty yellow industrial floor markings, restrained cold turquoise from electronics, soft bounced volumetric light and subtle scuffs. Cohesive warm palette and human scale, readable silhouette at thumbnail size. Visible characters, if any, are stylized blocky game figures with believable workwear. Render a single composed scene, no contact sheet, no decorative borders, no HUD, no watermark or signatures.
